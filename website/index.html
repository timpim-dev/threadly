Email, simplified.

Threadly organizes your inbox into threaded conversations and clubs, hiding the noise and showing you what matters.

Features

  • Local First: Emails are fetched via IMAP and stored encrypted on your device. No middleman servers.
  • Threaded Conversations: Automatically groups emails into logical threads, removing cluttered signatures.
  • Clubs: Automatically organizes newsletters and group emails into distinct, easy-to-read clubs.
  • Optional AI: Bring your own OpenRouter API key for on-device summaries and smart replies (opt-in only).
